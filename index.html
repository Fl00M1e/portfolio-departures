01 — PROFILE

About me

I've spent the last few years focused on the part of a website people actually touch: the layout, the interactions, the small details that make something feel finished instead of assembled.

I work well both from a finished Figma file and from a rough brief — if there's no design yet, I'll structure the page myself around what it needs to do and who it's for.

Code I hand over is meant to be picked up by someone else without a walkthrough: consistent naming, components that do one thing, and no hidden logic buried three files deep.
